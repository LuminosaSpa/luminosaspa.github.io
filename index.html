Outside of our scope (what we don’t do):
  • Role definition & authority: Determination of compliance or non-compliance
  • Training & workforce safety: Conducting safety training, certifying employees or operators
  • Incident, injury & event documentation: Filling out forms and submitting reports to OSHA or insurers
  • Inspections & regulator interaction: Conducting safety inspections, speaking with OSHA or regulators
  • Compliance & regulatory guidance: Interpretation of OSHA standards or laws, advising on compliance strategy
