Are You Experiencing This?

These are the patterns we see most often in warehouse and logistics operations.

Documentation Chaos

No one knows what’s current

  • Versions conflict across folders and emails
  • Inconsistent labeling and dating
  • Slow retrieval when someone needs a record fast
Audit & Insurance Panic

Scramble mode under pressure

  • Teams rush to locate files
  • Gaps appear at the worst time
  • Stress spikes during audits and reviews
1 Person With Knowledge

Knowledge lives in a head or inbox

  • Key documentation depends on one person
  • Risk if they’re out, promoted, or leave
  • No consistent handoff or structure
Blurred Responsibility

Everyone assumes someone else has it

  • Safety, HR, and operations each assume ownership
  • No single system or standard process
  • Documentation becomes reactive
Lack of Ongoing Upkeep

Records don’t reflect real-time changes

  • New hires not reflected consistently
  • Incidents and near-misses not logged reliably
  • Training/inspection records get lost over time
System Stress

Documentation becomes a bottleneck

  • Ops slows down to search for records
  • Uncertainty about what’s “official”
  • Unclear visibility across leadership

Documentation Intake & Organization

  • Secure digital intake and document upload process

System of Record Management

One authoritative source for all documentation

  • SharePoint-based document system of record
  • Standardized folder architecture across all categories
  • Version control and file dating standards

  • Controlled access and permissions for client users
Documentation Review & Gap Visibility

Clarity without judgment or enforcement

  • Review of documentation for completeness and currency
  • Identification of missing, outdated, or incomplete records
  • Neutral gap summaries (non-advisory)

  • Ongoing tracking of documentation status

What We Don’t Do

  • Role definition & authority: Determination of compliance or non-compliance

  • Compliance & regulatory guidance: Interpretation of OSHA standards or laws, advising on compliance strategy
